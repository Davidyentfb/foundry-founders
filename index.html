The Order

Forged in fire, bound to service

The Foundry — a fraternal Order operating as a charitable trust devoted to public service.

“As iron is shaped in the fire of a foundry, so members are shaped through discipline, learning, and service, and in turn help shape their communities.”
Charitable Purposes
  • Youth mentorship, education, and workforce development
  • Financial literacy and wealth-building education
  • Relief of poverty and hardship in the community
  • Scholarships and support for education and the trades

Token Distribution

How tokens are issued

Tokens are never published on this portal. A founder issues each member the token for their branch only — privately, by direct message or in person. A member receives one token, for one branch. If a token is ever compromised, the founders rotate it and the new token replaces the old across the portal in a single edit.

Documentation Standards

What every submission must meet

The standard is uniform across branches, so a record taken in once can be recognized everywhere in the house.

Identity & Authority

  • Legal names exactly as they appear on government or formation documents
  • Only the last four characters of any ID number on this portal — full documents move privately after intake
  • Business submissions must come from a person authorized to bind the entity
  • One current email and one current phone per record — the founders reply to these

Accuracy & Review

  • Every figure stated — revenue, audience, ownership — must be supportable on request
  • Ownership percentages across all owners must total 100%
  • Incomplete or inconsistent records are returned for completion before review
  • Submission is an application, not an acceptance; terms are set only in signed writing
